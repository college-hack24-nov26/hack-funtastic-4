Meet MythoVerse

Learning shaped by stories

MythoVerse aims to make cultural learning welcoming and engaging. Students will be able to explore mythology-inspired stories and ideas through age-aware explanations and activities.

The platform is being planned around meaningful learning, not finished features.

01

Stories with context

Introduce mythology-inspired stories with clear, considerate context for young learners.

02

Build toward activities and games that invite students to reflect, explore and learn by doing.

03

Learning at the learner’s pace

Plan learning experiences that can be tailored to a student’s age group and growing understanding.
