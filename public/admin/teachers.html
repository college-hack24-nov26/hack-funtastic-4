Gurukul curriculum

Teacher management

Edit teacher profiles and complete age-specific syllabuses as JSON. Drafts stay private; publishing requires all five age paths, chapter assessments, and a reviewed content status.

Content structure guide

Teacher profile fields include name, slug, identityType, title, shortIntroduction, mythologicalBackground, personality, teachingPhilosophy, teachingDomain, knowledgeAndSkills, biography, teachingStyle, portrait, sources, and contentReviewStatus.

Each ageGroups item has ageGroup, difficulty, learningObjectives, estimatedMinutes, and modules. Modules contain chapters; each chapter contains lessons and an assessment. Assessment questions keep correctAnswer and acceptedAnswers private until server-graded submission.
