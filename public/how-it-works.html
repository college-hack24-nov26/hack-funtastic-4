The planned student journey

How MythoVerse is planned to work

A guided path from setting up a learner profile to exploring stories and reflecting on progress. These steps describe the vision, not features that are available today.

MythoVerse is in development. Student registration, dashboard and learning modules are not available yet.

One step at a time

A learning journey with room to grow

  1. 01

    Registration

    A student account is intended to provide a starting point for an individual learning experience.

  2. 02

    Choose an age group

    The planned journey begins by selecting an age group so explanations and activities can be age-aware.

  3. 03

    Create a student profile

    A learner profile is intended to help organize their preferences and learning journey.

  4. 04

    Begin a learning journey

    Students will be able to explore a sequence of mythology- inspired topics at a suitable pace.

  5. 05

    Explore stories and activities

    Stories and future activities are planned to help students discover themes and ideas.

  6. 06

    Reflect and assess learning

    Learning checks are intended to encourage reflection and help students review what they have understood.

  7. 07

    Review progress

    Progress tracking is a planned way for learners to see their journey over time.

Built with learners in mind

A vision, still becoming reality

Each part of this journey is a platform goal. We will share more as the experience is developed.
