About MythoVerse

A future learning space where young people can encounter the richness of Indian mythology through stories, activities and play.

What we are building

Culture, curiosity and learning

MythoVerse is an educational gaming platform in development. Its purpose is to make mythology-inspired learning accessible, engaging and appropriate for students at different stages.

Stories from Indian traditions carry diverse characters, perspectives and ideas. We want learners to approach them with curiosity, care and room to ask questions.

Who it is for

A place for school-age learners

MythoVerse is intended for students across different age groups. Age-appropriate content and clear learning paths are part of the goal; exact age ranges and experiences are still to be defined.

We hope parents, guardians and educators can help students make thoughtful connections as they learn.
